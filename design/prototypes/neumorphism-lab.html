:hover sets --d: 1 and :active sets --p: 1 (pure CSS). A pointermove handler writes the cursor position as --px/--py in −1…1. Shadows and tilt are calc()s of those four numbers, registered with @property so they ease. Touch input is ignored, so phones get the plain sink.
